# 📊 Cosmetics Sales Data Analysis (Excel Project)

An end-to-end Excel project demonstrating **data cleaning, pivot table analysis, and interactive dashboard creation** using cosmetics sales data.

---

## 📁 Project Overview

This project analyzes sales performance across different countries and products to extract meaningful business insights. It showcases how Excel can be used for transforming raw data into actionable information.

### 🔍 Key Components:
- 🧹 Data Cleaning
- 📊 Pivot Table Analysis
- 📈 Dashboard Creation
- 📌 Business Insights

---

## 🧾 Dataset Description

The dataset contains sales records with the following fields:

- Sales Person  
- Country  
- Product  
- Date  
- Amount ($)  
- Boxes Shipped  
- Product Price  
- Demand Category (Low, Avg, Good, High)  

---

## 🧹 Data Cleaning (Working Sheet)

- Cleaned and structured raw data  
- Ensured proper formatting for dates and currency  
- Removed inconsistencies and validated entries  
- Created additional columns:
  - Product Price  
  - Demand Category  
- Prepared dataset for analysis  

---

## 📊 Pivot Table Analysis

Pivot Tables were used to summarize and analyze the dataset:

### 🌍 Country-wise Sales
- Aggregated total sales by country  
- Compared regional performance  

### 🧴 Product-wise Sales
- Identified top-performing products  
- Compared sales across product categories  

### 📉 Demand Analysis
- Segmented demand into:
  - Low Demand  
  - Avg Demand  
  - Good Demand  
  - High Demand  
- Analyzed product demand patterns  

---

## 📈 Dashboard

An interactive dashboard was created to visualize insights effectively.

### ✨ Features:
- Total Sales by Product (Bar Chart)  
- Demand Distribution by Country (Pie Chart)  
- Demand Trends (Line Chart)  
- Interactive Filters:
  - Product  
  - Demand Category  

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Australia and USA contribute significantly to total sales  
- Anti-Aging Serum and Body Butter Cream are among top-performing products  
- High Demand category contributes the most to overall sales  
- Sales distribution varies across countries and product types
